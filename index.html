Process

From “I think I know what I mean” to “that’s exactly it.”

Good communication starts before the writing.

First, we figure out what you’re actually trying to say, who needs to hear it, and what they need to understand. The words come after.

01

Discover

I ask the useful questions. Sometimes the annoying ones.

What are you really trying to say? What’s getting buried? What does your audience understand already, and what keeps getting lost?

02

Shape the message

Strategy, messaging, copy, content, or creative direction, depending on what the project needs.

Everything builds from the same place: the idea worth paying attention to.

03

Deliver

Then I make it work.

Clear, credible, recognizably you. No unnecessary polishing. No pretending to be louder or bigger than you are.

About The Good Bit

Hi, I’m Cam.

My job is finding the good bit: the idea at the center of the work that has some life in it, then building the message around that.

I started The Good Bit because most communication problems are clarity problems. When you know what you stand for and what you’re trying to say, the rest gets easier.

I’m an interdisciplinary creative with a background across writing, strategy, photography, music, and digital communication.

I work across brand voice, messaging, content, editorial strategy, and digital presence, helping people communicate complicated, interesting, or genuinely useful work without sanding all the personality off it.
