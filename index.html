About The Good Bit

Hi, I'm Cam.

I started The Good Bit because most communication problems are actually clarity problems.

When you're clear on who you are, what you stand for, and what you want to say, saying it well becomes a lot easier. That's where I come in: finding the good bit. The idea at the center of your work that has a pulse, and is genuinely worth sharing.

I'm an interdisciplinary creative with a background across writing, strategy, photography, music, and digital communication. From there, I help shape everything else: brand voice, messaging, content, and digital presence — not by being louder, but by being clearer.

FAQ

Things you might be wondering.

Straight answers. No fog machine.

✈︎Curious minds welcome.
01 / FAQIs The Good Bit just you?
Yes. I run The Good Bit as a small digital marketing studio. I lead the strategy, messaging, writing, and creative direction, so you work directly with me. For larger or more specialised projects, I bring in trusted collaborators.
02 / FAQDo you work with agencies and other studios?
Yes. I work directly with clients, and I also collaborate with agencies, studios, and creative teams that need support with strategy, copywriting, messaging, content, editorial work, or social media. Some projects are led by The Good Bit. Others are collaborations or specialist contributions inside a wider team. Either way, the role is always clear.
03 / FAQWhat kind of work do you do?
Strategy, messaging, copywriting, content, social media, editorial planning, campaign ideas, brand voice, and creative direction. I can also support web presence and landing page projects, especially around messaging, structure, copy, and creative direction. For design and development, I collaborate with trusted specialists when needed.
04 / FAQWho do you usually work with?
Thoughtful brands, founders, researchers, creatives, startups, small teams, mission-driven businesses, and agencies that need sharp thinking and clear words. Basically: people with good ideas who do not want those ideas flattened into generic marketing paste.
05 / FAQHow does a project usually work?
I start with a discovery call, then define the scope, deliverables, timeline, and working process. From there, I usually move through strategy, messaging, creation, feedback, and final delivery. Simple enough to follow. Structured enough to avoid chaos goblin mode.
06 / FAQDo you offer ongoing support?
Yes. Ongoing support is available for content, social media, campaigns, messaging, editorial planning, and general communication support. This can be direct client work or support for another studio, agency, or internal team.
07 / FAQDo you only work with clients in one country?
No. I work internationally. I am often moving between places and time zones, but the process stays clear. Calls, timelines, and communication are agreed upfront so nobody has to guess where anything stands.
